PrayerRug Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 1, 2026

PrayerRug helps you keep your prayers on time. It blocks the apps you choose during prayer windows until you scan your prayer rug, and it can wake you for Fajr. We built it to work with as little of your data as possible. This page explains exactly what the app uses and why.

What stays on your phone

These things are processed only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, except where noted below.

  • Camera. When you scan your rug, the camera image is analyzed on your phone to recognize the rug. Images are never saved and never leave your device.
  • Location. Your location is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is stored on your phone and is not sent to us. To show the name of your city, the app asks Apple's location service once, which receives your approximate location. Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Blocked apps. You choose apps to block through Apple's Screen Time system. Apple gives PrayerRug only anonymous tokens for them, so we cannot see which apps you chose. Your choices stay on your phone.
  • Prayer history. Your marked prayers and weekly stats are stored on your phone.

Free trial

To remember when your free trial started, the app saves that date and the trial length in your phone's Keychain and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account. This way the trial does not start over after a reinstall or on a new phone. We cannot read this data.

Usage analytics

To understand how people use the app and improve it, PrayerRug sends a small number of anonymous events:

  • when blocking is set up;
  • when a rug is scanned;
  • when the subscription screen is shown;
  • when a purchase is made.

Each event includes a random identifier created by the app, the event name and the app version. Depending on the event, it may also include which prayer it was, the day of your free trial, or the plan you bought (monthly or yearly). The time is recorded when the event arrives. The identifier is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier. If you delete and reinstall the app, a new identifier is created.

These events are stored in a spreadsheet we keep in Google Workspace. Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. Like any web service, Google may see your IP address when an event is sent. It is not saved in our spreadsheet.

We do not use analytics for advertising. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or track you across other apps and websites.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active.

App settings

The app downloads a small settings file from GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when the file is downloaded. We do not receive this data.

How long we keep data

Anonymous analytics events are kept for as long as they help us improve the app, and no longer than 24 months. Data on your phone is removed when you delete the app. The free trial start date stays in your Keychain and iCloud, so the trial does not restart.

Children

PrayerRug is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

You can turn off camera and location access at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Since analytics are anonymous, we cannot link them to you. If you have a question or request about your data, write to us and we will help.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
