PrayerRug Support

Something not working? Most answers are below. If not, write to us at PrayerRug.help@gmail.com and we will reply within 2 business days.

The scanner doesn't recognize my rug

Step back so the whole rug fits in the frame. Add more light or turn on the flashlight with the button on the scanner screen. Hold the phone steady for a moment.

How do I stop the Fajr alarm?

Scan your prayer rug. The alarm keeps ringing until you do. To stop using the alarm, turn off Fajr alarm in the app's Settings. The change starts from the next morning.

The Fajr alarm didn't ring

Open iPhone Settings, find PrayerRug and make sure alarms are allowed. Check that the Ringtones and Alerts volume in Sounds and Haptics is not at the minimum. If headphones are connected, the alarm may play through them.

A blocked app won't open

That's the point. Scan your rug and your apps open again. If you really need an app right now, tap Pause on the block screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then PrayerRug. Apple's guide: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com

I bought a subscription but it's not active

Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore.
